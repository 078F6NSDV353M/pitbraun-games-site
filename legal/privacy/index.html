3. Information We Process

When you access the website, certain technical information may be processed automatically to deliver the requested pages and files, maintain security, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse.

This information may include:

  • IP address;
  • date and time of a request;
  • requested page, file, or resource;
  • referring page or website;

  • device type;
  • operating-system information;

  • error and diagnostic information;

3.2 Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve its content, structure, performance, and browser games.

Depending on the configuration and the way you use the website, Google Analytics may process:

  • pages viewed;
  • session information;
  • general interaction events;
  • referring websites or traffic sources;
  • browser and device characteristics;
  • screen resolution;
  • approximate geographic information;
  • pseudonymous identifiers stored through cookies or similar technologies;
  • other technical information required to provide the analytics service.

Google Analytics may receive an IP address at collection time for purposes such as routing, service operation, spam detection, and deriving approximate geographic information.

Google states that raw IP addresses are discarded after use and are not made available to PitBraun Games through Google Analytics reports.

PitBraun Games does not intentionally send names, email addresses, message contents, or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics.

3.3 Information you provide

If you contact PitBraun Games by email or through another contact method provided on the website, we may process the information you choose to provide.

This may include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the content of your message;
  • attachments you send;
  • other information you voluntarily include in your communication.

3.4 Browser-game information

Browser games available through the website may use localStorage or similar browser storage to save information locally on your device.

This information may include:

  • high scores;
  • game progress;
  • sound preferences;
  • gameplay settings;
  • other local game preferences.

Information stored through localStorage normally remains on your device and is not transmitted to PitBraun Games unless a particular game or feature expressly states otherwise.
