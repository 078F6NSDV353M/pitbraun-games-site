1. Scope of this Policy

2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser when you visit a website.

Cookies may be used to remember information, distinguish users or sessions, maintain security, provide website functionality, or measure how a website is used.

Some cookies are removed when a browser session ends, while others may remain on the device until they expire or are manually deleted.

3. Technologies Used on the Website

PitBraun Games may use the following types of browser storage and similar technologies:

  • cookies;
  • localStorage;
  • session storage where required;
  • browser caching technologies;
  • similar technologies used by website service providers.

4. Essential and Security Technologies

Certain technologies may be necessary to deliver the website, maintain security, prevent abuse, manage network traffic, and provide requested functionality.

The website is delivered through Cloudflare. Depending on the Cloudflare services and security features active for a particular request, Cloudflare may use cookies or similar technologies for security and service operation.

These technologies may be used without optional analytics functionality where they are necessary to provide or protect the website.

More information is available in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy .

5. Google Analytics

PitBraun Games uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website and to improve website content, performance, navigation, and browser games.

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies to distinguish users and maintain session information.

Google Analytics cookies

  • _ga — used to distinguish users. The default expiration period configured by Google Analytics is two years.
  • _ga_<container-id> — used to maintain session state. The default expiration period configured by Google Analytics is two years.

Actual cookie lifetime may be shorter because of browser restrictions, user settings, deletion, consent settings, or changes made by Google.

Google Analytics may also process technical and usage information as described in the Privacy Policy.

More information is available in Google's documentation about Google Analytics cookie usage .

6. Consent for Analytics

Where applicable law requires consent before non-essential analytics technologies are used, PitBraun Games will request the relevant choice before enabling such storage or processing.

Where a consent mechanism is provided, your analytics preference may be communicated to Google through supported consent controls.

You may withdraw or change consent where the website provides a consent-management control.

7. Browser-Game Storage

Browser games on pitbraun.games may use localStorage or similar browser storage to retain game information on your device.

Depending on the game, this may include:

  • high scores;
  • game progress;
  • sound settings;
  • control preferences;
  • other gameplay settings.

Browser-game localStorage is normally stored only on your device and is not transmitted to PitBraun Games unless a particular game or feature expressly states otherwise.

Removing local browser storage may permanently remove locally saved game information.

8. How to Control Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies and other stored website data.

Browser controls may allow you to:

  • delete existing cookies;
  • block all cookies;
  • block third-party cookies;
  • configure different rules for individual websites;
  • clear localStorage and other site data.

Blocking or deleting essential website storage may affect certain functionality.

Deleting browser-game storage may reset high scores, preferences, progress, or other locally stored game information.

Their technologies, retention periods, and practices may change independently of PitBraun Games.
