Privacy Policy

Bubbles

Last updated: September 3, 2026

PitBraun Games built Bubbles as a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Bubbles application.

1. Personal Data

Bubbles does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

The application does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information.

2. Local Game Data

Bubbles may store limited game data locally on your device, such as:

  • High scores
  • Game settings
  • Sound preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to PitBraun Games or third parties.

3. Advertising

Bubbles does not currently display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Bubbles does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising tracking, or behavioral tracking services.

5. Location

Bubbles does not access, collect, or use your precise or approximate location.

6. Device Permissions

Bubbles does not request access to sensitive device data such as contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

7. Internet Connection

The core gameplay of Bubbles does not require an internet connection.

The application does not send gameplay or personal data to PitBraun Games servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Bubbles does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the application does not collect personal data, PitBraun Games does not maintain personal profiles of children or other users.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version of Bubbles does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, social networking, or account services that collect personal information through the application.

10. Future Changes

Future versions of Bubbles may introduce additional features, such as optional in-app purchases, online services, advertising, or analytics.

If such features change how user data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before or when those features are released.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

PitBraun Games may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact:
